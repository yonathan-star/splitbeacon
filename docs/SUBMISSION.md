**Title:** SplitBeacon

**Week:** Week 4 — Displays

**Requested tier:** Tier 3 target

**Description:** SplitBeacon is a standalone lap and interval timer that makes pacing visible. A 64-pixel RGB display shows progress and split history while a 240x135 TFT shows precise timing, targets and session statistics. Three physical buttons record laps, pause the session and switch views. Target and brightness knobs, ambient dimming and piezo cues make it usable without a phone. The package includes an original controller PCB, protected power design, MicroPython firmware, a printable enclosure, assembly instructions and a vendor-priced BOM with actual cart screenshots.

**Why this tier:** The scope integrates electrical, mechanical and software design: a routed custom board, two display types, analog sensing, independent RGB data channels, a stateful timing interface, persistence and a serviceable case. Tier is a requested target, subject to review.
