**Cart reconciliation:** Adafruit $74.55 + DigiKey $10.26 + Polymaker $18.99 = **$103.80 in purchased parts/material**. DigiKey shows estimated shipping $4.99 and tariff $0.54, total $15.79. Adafruit and Polymaker shipping/tax are calculated at checkout and are not zero-cost promises.

**PCB fabrication is additional.** Live JLCPCB analysis accepted a 100x100mm, two-layer board. The saved screenshot is a dimension-based quote, not a saved PCB cart. The earlier upload was detected correctly; after sign-in navigation the upload was not retained. Initial web quote was $4 for five boards, with the displayed express shipping estimate $26.86. The $2 advertised price applies to its desktop app and is not used as the web quote. No PCB purchase was made.

Against a $100 grant, full-cart parts alone exceed funding by $3.80. With the initial PCB quote and known DigiKey charges, the known amount is $113.33 before other shipping/tax and PCB shipping. Reusing an existing filament spool lowers new parts purchases by $18.99, but ownership of a spool is not assumed.

Headers: Pico headers are included. Use only the three required pads per FeatherWing, cutting individual pins from the supplied header strips. The remaining included header pins supply controller J1-J8. TFT header is included with its breakout. No sockets are required.

Consumables/tools: solder, insulation for soldered wire joints, a soldering iron, multimeter, screwdriver, printer access and a USB data cable are workshop requirements. Their ownership/cost is not verified and they are not hidden as purchased BOM lines.
